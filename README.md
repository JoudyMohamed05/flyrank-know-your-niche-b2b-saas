# Know Your Niche: B2B SaaS

**Example business:** FlyRank  
**Audience:** founders, heads of marketing/content, ecommerce leaders, and agencies that need measurable organic and AI-search visibility.

## Market reality

B2B SaaS buyers self-educate before speaking to sales. G2 reports that eight in ten buyers use AI search in the buying process, while reviews remain a trust layer for both people and AI systems. LinkedIn and Edelman also report that more than 40% of B2B deals stall because buying groups are not aligned. FlyRank therefore has to win twice: **be discovered early** and **give the internal champion enough proof to convince everyone else**.

## Top 3 channels

### 1. AI search + software review ecosystems

**Where buyers are:** ChatGPT-style research, comparison queries, G2 reviews, “best tool” pages, and customer proof.  
**Why it converts:** Buyers use these sources to build shortlists before contacting vendors. Clear positioning, reviews, comparisons, and transparent proof make FlyRank easier to recommend and safer to shortlist.  
**Action this month:** Publish one fair “FlyRank vs. alternatives” page, add three evidence-rich customer stories, strengthen product/FAQ schema, request five verified reviews, and track a fixed set of 20 buyer prompts every two weeks.  
**KPI:** qualified demo visits and the percentage of monitored AI prompts that mention FlyRank.

### 2. LinkedIn founder-led thought leadership

**Where buyers are:** founder, marketing, SEO/content, ecommerce, and agency conversations. Hidden buyers also consume thought leadership even when they do not follow salespeople.  
**Why it converts:** B2B purchases involve groups. Useful point-of-view content gives a champion language and evidence to share internally.  
**Action this month:** Run a weekly three-post loop: (1) original benchmark, (2) customer teardown, (3) contrarian operator lesson. Each post should link to one proof asset, not a generic homepage.  
**KPI:** saves, comments from target roles, proof-asset visits, and influenced demos.

### 3. Trusted founder and agency communities + partners

**Where buyers are:** Indie Hackers, vetted marketing communities, agency networks, niche newsletters, podcasts, and adjacent SaaS ecosystems. Indie Hackers explicitly centers transparent founder stories and peer feedback.  
**Why it converts:** Trust transfers from a respected host, community, or partner. Agencies can also introduce FlyRank into multiple client accounts.  
**Action this month:** Publish one transparent experiment in a founder community, recruit two agency design partners, and pitch one co-hosted “AI visibility benchmark” session. No mass promotion or unsolicited DMs.  
**KPI:** benchmark participants, partner-introduced opportunities, and qualified conversations.

## The #1 challenge: proving value across a skeptical buying group

The hard part is not generating awareness; it is reducing perceived risk. SEO and AI visibility gains take time, attribution is imperfect, and “AI-powered” claims are easy to copy. A marketing lead may like FlyRank while finance wants ROI, leadership wants predictability, and IT wants transparency. The answer is **proof packaging**: baseline metrics, clear methodology, time-to-first-value, customer evidence, security/data answers, and a small low-risk starting offer.

## Where AI helps

- **Research:** summarize interview notes, reviews, sales calls, competitor pages, and community questions into objections and message themes.
- **Content production:** turn one verified benchmark into a LinkedIn post, comparison page, email, sales slide, and webinar outline while preserving the source evidence.
- **Personalization:** draft account-specific teardown hypotheses from public data; a human checks every claim before outreach.
- **GEO monitoring:** run recurring buyer prompts, record whether FlyRank is mentioned, identify cited sources, and recommend missing proof/content.
- **Sales enablement:** generate role-specific one-pagers for the marketer, finance lead, executive sponsor, and technical reviewer.
- **Quality control:** flag unsupported claims, vague AI language, inconsistent numbers, and content that lacks a measurable next step.

**Rule:** AI accelerates synthesis and execution; humans own strategy, factual verification, community judgment, and customer promises.

## 30-day starter plan

| Week | Move | Output |
|---|---|---|
